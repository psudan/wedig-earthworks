What we do

Earthworks that move your project forward.

From farms and vineyards to building sites, we take on the excavation work that has to be done properly before anything else can start.

Site preparation

Clearing, levelling and shaping ground ready for building, paving or planting.

Trenching

Trenches for irrigation, water and electrical lines, drainage and other services.

Landscaping & contouring

Shaping and grading land for farms, vineyards, estates and gardens.

Foundations & post holes

Foundation trenches and holes for fencing, trellising and farm structures.

Land clearing

Removing stumps, rocks, rubble and unwanted vegetation.

Access roads & tracks

Creating and repairing farm roads and site access tracks.
